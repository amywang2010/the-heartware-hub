04 The app

Record. Correct.
Or refuse.

This is the companion app, running in this page. Open the microphone and it will show you what your own chest wall does to a heart sound; press the generated signal and it will show you a grade 2 murmur through 34 mm, corrected, without a patient in the room.

Microphone audio never leaves the browser. Nothing is uploaded, stored or sent anywhere.

What the four screens do.

Record captures six seconds and stores the waveform, the wall estimate and the quality score together, so a reading is never separated from the conditions that produced it. Correct divides the spectrum by the wall it just measured. Keep exports the take as WAV and JSON, so a second reader hears the same sound rather than a description of it. Refuse returns nothing below the contact threshold: a false reassurance is the failure that hurts a patient.

The correction is the same arithmetic as the model in research/, at demo precision. There is no learned layer, so there is nothing in it to inherit a demographic from.

The firmware build adds the second microphone and the two-channel noise rejection. This page has one channel, so it shows the correction and the gate, not the differential front end.
