The method

The wall is a filter. Divide it out.

Five things happen between the chestpiece and the screen. Four of them are engineering. The fifth is the one that decides whether a clinician trusts the reading.

1

Acoustic capture

Two MEMS microphones look into the stock acoustic channel. The port capsule sits at the cup's own 3 mm port, so the sound path is the manufacturer's rather than one we invented.

2

Reference channel

The second capsule sits behind a sealed cavity on the same board. Room noise reaches both capsules; the heart reaches only one. Subtracting them rejects 6.0 dB of room noise before anything is stored.

3

Thoracic correction

The chest wall is a low-pass filter whose resonance falls as it thickens. From the recording alone, the insert estimates the wall to 3.7 mm RMS, then divides the murmur band by the filter that wall implies.

4

Analysis

Bands, timing and a quality score. No learned layer, so there is no training set to inherit a demographic from and nothing a reviewer has to take on trust.

5

Clinical interface

The reading is a recording, a wall estimate and a margin. It does not name a valve lesion, because naming one from a single channel has not been validated.

The arithmetic

One division, and nothing hidden inside it.

The correction has no parameters that were fitted to people. That is the claim, and it is checkable in an afternoon with the code.

Ĥ(f) = H(f) / T(f, d̂)

H is the recording. T is the transfer function the chest wall implies at the estimated thickness. The hat on d means estimated, and the estimate is the only uncertain thing in the line.

Limits

What this does not fix.

A page that claims everything is a page nobody believes. These are the things still open, in the order they worry us.

Diagnosis

It does not say what is wrong with the heart. Valve disease detection needs paired recordings with echocardiography, and that study has not been run.

Placement

A recording from the wrong intercostal space is still a recording from the wrong space. The app asks for the site and stores it; it does not correct for it.

Contact pressure

Pressure changes the low band. The gate refuses poor contact but the repeatability protocol across operators is still being written.

The physical model

It is a model, fitted to four thicknesses. It holds in simulation and on the bench. It has not been checked against imaging in people.
