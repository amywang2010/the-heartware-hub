Work with us

Three ways in, all of them small.

A clinic, a distributor, a manufacturer. Each has its own first step, and none of them needs a commitment before either of us knows whether this works.

01

Run a pilot.

A clinic or trial site takes twenty to forty inserts on loan, with the protocol, the recording template and the analysis. You record patients who already have an echo booked, before the echo is read. We write up what happened, including the parts that did not work.

  • No fee, and no exclusivity.
  • One named owner at your end.
  • Result publishable either way.
Pilot, in one line →

02

Sell it to clinics.

Five demo units and a price list, with the list of tested heads. You take them to the accounts you already call on and tell us what the objection is.

  • No first-year minimum.
  • Territory discussed once it sells.
  • Demos stay with you either way.

03

Build it into a head.

You have the tooling, the supply chain and the regulatory experience. We have the part, the method and the bench evidence for it. The conversation starts with the design files, not with a valuation.

  • Design files and bench data first.
  • NDA before any CAD.
