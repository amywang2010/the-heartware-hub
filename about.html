About

It started with two recordings that should have matched.

Same murmur, same grade, two patients. One was audible at the door, one needed the room quiet and the chestpiece pressed hard. The difference was not the heart. It was the tissue in front of it.

Why a physical part, and not an app

The chest wall is a filter, and it sits between the heart and every microphone you could put on the skin. Software can correct it only if it knows what the filter was, and the only place that information exists is the recording itself. So the device measures the wall, and the software divides by it.

Why it is not a model

A learned correction would need recordings paired with imaging from thousands of bodies, and it would be impossible to say which part of the answer came from the physics and which from the training set. This is one division, and it can be checked with a calculator.
