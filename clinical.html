In the clinic

The same heart, on any chest.

A stethoscope is the cheapest test in medicine and the one most sensitive to who is holding it and who is lying under it. The insert takes the second variable out.

What comes back

A recording, a wall estimate and a murmur level. Not a description of what someone thought they heard.

The quiet patient

A thick chest stops reading as a quiet murmur, so the same lesion sounds similar in a 60 kg and a 120 kg body.

The handover

The next clinician hears the sound itself, with the site and quality attached, instead of a sentence.

The refusal

Poor contact returns nothing. The instrument is allowed to say it did not get a reading.

The ward round

The stethoscope in the pocket is the one that gets the insert. No cart, no network, no change to the round.

Five minutes

Threaded in by hand at the sink, then it is part of the instrument for the rest of the shift.

One protocol, one room, twenty patients.

We bring

  • Twenty to forty inserts, on loan for the length of the pilot.
  • The protocol, the recording template and the analysis.
  • A person on the phone when something does not work.

You need

  • Enough echo-booked patients to reach twenty.
  • One person to own it, and a room to record in.
  • Permission to publish the result, whichever way it goes.

No fee, no exclusivity, and no requirement that the result be flattering. If it does not work, that is worth knowing in month one.
