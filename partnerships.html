Partnerships

Three ways to
work with us.

A pilot site, a distribution partner, or a manufacturer who wants the insert as a factory variant. Each is a different conversation and each has a different next step.

01 · CLINICAL

Run the study

A site that already performs echocardiography records the same patient twice — corrected and uncorrected — and keeps the echo report. We supply the inserts, the protocol and the analysis; the site supplies the room and the reads.

Needs: one clinician lead, an echo list, and a data agreement.

02 · DISTRIBUTION

Sell the part

Regional distributors and group purchasing organisations already sell stethoscopes and gloves into the same rooms. The insert is a consumable decision at $59 rather than a capital request, with no per-exam fee to explain.

Needs: a pilot reference site and a price list.

03 · OEM

Ship it as a variant

Because the insert replaces a back plate rather than changing the instrument, a stethoscope manufacturer can offer it without redesigning a head. A royalty per unit is the only path to six-figure volume without a salesforce.

Needs: a tooling partner and a licensing term sheet.

What a pilot involves

Twenty to forty patients who are already scheduled for an echocardiogram. Each is recorded with and without correction, blinded to the echo result. The analysis reports whether the correction changes the finding, and how often the app refuses to answer at all — both outcomes are results, and the second is the one that protects patients.

What we bring. Inserts for the site, the recording protocol, the analysis and the write-up. What we ask. A named clinical lead and access to the echo report.

Commercial

Three revenue lines,
in order of realism.

A device has to be worth buying once before anything recurring is credible, so the first line is the only one that matters this year.

YEAR 1–2

Units

Sold through medical distributors and directly to pilot sites, at $59 with the app included. A clinic already buys stethoscopes; this is a line on an existing order.

YEAR 2+

Clinic tier

Multi-user records, trend views and EMR export, sold per site rather than per exam. Per-exam pricing is what stops a screening tool being used in a ten-minute visit.

YEAR 3+

OEM licence

A royalty per unit with a stethoscope manufacturer shipping the insert as a factory variant. This is where the volume is, and it is why the design fits a stock head.

The first hundred people who held it asked for the same thing: proof that the correction changes what a clinician hears. That is the pilot, and it is the only thing standing between the design and the shelf.
