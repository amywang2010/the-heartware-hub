Work with us

Three conversations, none of them a pitch deck.

Pick the one that matches you. Each is a small first step, and each says what it costs before you ask.

Run a pilot in your clinic20 to 40 patients

We bring twenty to forty inserts on loan, the protocol, the recording template and the analysis. You record patients who already have an echo booked, before the echo is read. Then we write up what happened, including the parts that did not work.

No fee, no exclusivity, and no requirement that the result be flattering. If it does not work, you should know in month one, and so should we.

hello@heart-ware.org with the word pilot in the subject line.

Sell it to clinicsfive demo units

We ship five demo units and a price list. You take them to the accounts you already call on, and tell us what the objection is. There is no first-year minimum, because a minimum would make us ship to a shelf instead of to a room.

If it sells, we talk about territory. If it does not, you keep the demos and we learn why.

hello@heart-ware.org with the territories you cover.

What a director needs to know.

Cost, compatibility, installation, training and regulatory status on one page, because those are the five questions that get asked first.

Unit cost$38.25, app included.
Compatibility44, 46 and 48 mm single and double heads. A list of tested models ships with the units.
Capital equipmentNone. It fits the stethoscope the clinic already owns.
InstallationFive minutes, threaded by hand. No tool and no adhesive.
TrainingOne session. The protocol is a page long.
Regulatory statusPrototype. Not a medical device, not cleared, not for diagnosis.

What we will not do.

  • Claim it finds disease. It corrects a recording, and a recording is not a diagnosis.
  • Put a unit in a clinic without a protocol and a named lead. That is how a pilot becomes a drawer.
  • Give a date for tooling we have not paid for.
