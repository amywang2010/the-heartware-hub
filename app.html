What it does at each step.

The interface is small on purpose. A clinician should be able to read the state of a recording from across a room, and should never have to guess whether a take was good.

ListeningA six second take on one channel. The waveform is drawn as it arrives, so a bad seal is visible before the take ends.
MeasuringThe wall estimate climbs as the take fills. No user input, no calibration step.
CorrectingThe gain applied to the murmur band, shown as a number rather than hidden in a settings menu.
AcceptingA quality score and a decision. Below 45 per cent contact the app returns nothing and asks for another take.
StoringWAV and JSON on the phone, exportable to the record the clinic already uses.
