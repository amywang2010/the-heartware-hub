Companion app

The recording app.

One screen for a six second take. It shows the waveform, chest wall estimate, contact score and corrected level. It does not grade a murmur or suggest a diagnosis.

  1. 01Six seconds, one channel, 20 Hz to 2 kHz. The waveform is drawn as it arrives.
  2. 02The chest wall is estimated from the recording. No tape measure, no input from the person holding it.
  3. 03The murmur band is divided by the wall it just measured. The result is the corrected murmur level.
  4. 04Contact below 45 per cent returns no result. The take is refused.

A generated grade 2 murmur through a modelled chest wall, played in the browser. It shows the interface and runs itself. It does not measure you.
