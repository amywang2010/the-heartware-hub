About

The instrument should
adapt to the body,
not the reverse.

Heartware began with a simple observation: the same murmur is described differently in different bodies, and the difference is in the instrument, not the ear.

Every commercial answer to this problem is a new instrument. A clinic that already owns a drawer of stethoscopes does not buy a fourth for a nurse who has ten minutes. So the design started from the constraint instead: change the back plate, keep everything else.

The second decision was to make the correction arithmetic rather than learned. A model with no trained parameters cannot encode the demographics of whoever happened to be in a dataset, and a reviewer can check it with a pencil. In a field where algorithmic bias is a stated concern, that is the whole point.

The third was the refusal. The strongest feedback from the clinicians who handled the prototype was that a false reassurance is worse than no reading, so the app returns nothing below its quality threshold and asks for another recording.

STAGE

Prototype

A working insert, recordings from people, an app that returns gated findings. The design is at revision D and the next milestone is the paired study, which is what the pilot section is for.

WHAT WE NEED

A clinical site with echocardiography

Validation, regulatory advice, manufacturing tooling, and distribution into public systems. None of those is solved by money alone, which is why the ask is specific.

More than a hundred people — students, nurses, physicians and volunteers — have handled the unit or given structured feedback. Their input changed three things: the insert became a threaded retrofit rather than a clip, the findings moved from a single flag to a site plus a likelihood, and the app gained its refusal state.

Twenty to forty patients already booked for an echo is all we need to start.

The study is a paired recording: corrected and uncorrected, blinded, read against the cardiologist's report. Email the clinical lead and we will send the protocol and the two-page data agreement the same day.
