In the clinic

The same heart, on any chest.

A stethoscope is the cheapest test in medicine and the one most sensitive to who is holding it and who is lying under it. The insert takes the second variable out.

What changes at the bedside

The reading

A recording, a wall estimate and a margin. Not a description of what someone thought they heard.

The quiet patient

A thick chest wall no longer reads as a quiet murmur, so the same lesion sounds similar in a 60 kg and a 120 kg body.

The handover

The next clinician hears the sound itself, with the site and quality attached, instead of a sentence.

The refusal

Poor contact returns nothing. The instrument is allowed to say it did not get a reading.

Where it gets used

Ward round

The stethoscope in the pocket is the one that gets the insert.

Outpatient clinic

A recording survives the appointment and can be reviewed later.

Outreach

No network needed. Capture and storage are on the phone.

The pilot

One protocol, one room, twenty patients.

Enough to answer the only question that matters at this stage: does the corrected recording agree with the echo that was already ordered?
