If you run the budget

What a director needs to know.

Cost, compatibility, installation, training and regulatory status on one screen, because those are the five questions that get asked first.

Unit cost

$38.25 in parts at 500 units. $59 a pilot unit, app included.

Compatibility

44, 46 and 48 mm single and double heads. A list of tested models ships with the units.

Capital equipment

None. It fits the stethoscope the clinic already owns.

Installation

Five minutes, threaded by hand, no tool and no adhesive.

Training

One session. The protocol is a page long.

Regulatory status

Prototype. Not a medical device, not cleared, not for diagnosis. The route is not chosen yet.

What we will not do

  • Claim it finds disease. It corrects a recording.
  • Put a unit in a clinic without a protocol and a named lead.
  • Give a date for tooling we have not paid for.
